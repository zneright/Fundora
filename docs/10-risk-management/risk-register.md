## 1. Risk Assessment Methodology

Each technical, security, operational, and schedule risk is evaluated by:
- **Severity:** `Critical` | `High` | `Medium` | `Low`
- **Likelihood:** `High` | `Medium` | `Low`
- **Impact:** Concrete effect on security, funds, user trust, or hackathon delivery.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Architectural and engineering controls implemented in Fundora.
- **Verification Method:** How the mitigation is tested and proven.

## 3. Phase 1 & Phase 2 Risk Verification Status

- **`RISK-01` (Escrow Refund Misrouting / Unauthorized Release):** **MITIGATED & VERIFIED IN PHASE 2** — `refund_project` transfers `remaining_balance_yocto` strictly to `project.funder_treasury`, and quorum payout requires `total_votes >= min_votes` and `(votes_approve * 10000) >= (total_votes * quorum_bps)`. Verified by `sc_test_04`, `sc_test_05`, `sc_test_06`, and `sandbox_e2e_multi_account_escrow_and_callback_simulation`.
- **`RISK-03` (Sybil & Cross-Barangay Voting Manipulation):** **MITIGATED & VERIFIED IN PHASE 2** — `is_verified_resident` and `vote_milestone` strictly check `barangay.verified_residents.contains(&voter)` (defaulting to `false`), block self-voting by `sk_official` and `barangay_admin`, and enforce `min_votes >= 3` for all standard barangays. Verified by `sc_test_01`, `sc_test_02`, and `sc_test_05`.
- **`RISK-05` (Asynchronous Cross-Shard Transfer Callback Failure):** **MITIGATED & VERIFIED IN PHASE 2** — `PayoutInFlight` and `RefundInFlight` reserve funds in `reserved_in_flight_yocto`; if `on_transfer_complete` or `on_refund_complete` observes `PromiseResult::Failed`, funds are restored to `remaining_balance_yocto`, status transitions to `ReconciliationRequired`, and `retry_failed_milestone_payout` / `retry_failed_refund` enable safe recovery. Verified in `sc_test_04`, `sc_test_06`, and `sandbox_integration.rs`.
- **`RISK-07` & `RISK-08` (Firestore Rules & Zero On-Chain PII):** **MITIGATED & VERIFIED IN PHASES 1–2** — On-chain structs in `models.rs` store only pseudonymous NEAR `AccountId`s, `psgc_code`, and 64-character SHA-256 hex commitments (`metadata_hash`, `proof_hash`, `report_sha256_hash`).
